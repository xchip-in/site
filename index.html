XCHIP.IN

XChip is a technology company at the intersection of artificial intelligence and semiconductor innovation. We design specialized AI accelerators and intelligent computing systems that power next-generation machine learning applications across industries.

The AI revolution demands more than general-purpose computing. From edge inference to large-scale model training, modern AI workloads require purpose-built hardware optimized for neural networks, transformers, and emerging architectures. We're reimagining silicon design to unlock AI's full potential—making it faster, more efficient, and accessible to everyone.

We are a team of AI researchers, chip architects, and systems engineers dedicated to building the computational foundation for intelligent systems that will shape tomorrow.

AI-first hardware design

Purpose-built for intelligence. Generic processors leave performance on the table. Our chips are architected specifically for AI workloads, with optimized tensor operations, memory hierarchies, and dataflow patterns that accelerate inference and training by orders of magnitude.

Efficient AI everywhere

Edge AI capabilities. We bring AI processing to the edge—enabling real-time inference on devices without cloud dependency. Our low-power AI accelerators make intelligent applications possible on mobile devices, IoT sensors, and embedded systems.

Scalable AI infrastructure. From single-chip solutions to distributed training clusters, our architecture scales seamlessly. Whether you're deploying models on a smartphone or training foundation models across data centers, we provide the computational backbone.

Intelligence meets reliability

Production-ready AI systems. AI is only valuable when it works reliably in production. Our platforms are engineered for 24/7 operation with robust error handling, thermal management, and fault tolerance for mission-critical AI applications.

Developer-friendly AI tools. We provide comprehensive frameworks, compilers, and optimization tools that let developers focus on models—not hardware complexity. Support for PyTorch, TensorFlow, and popular ML frameworks is built in.

Privacy-preserving AI. In sensitive applications, data privacy is paramount. Our hardware supports secure enclaves, encrypted inference, and on-device learning—keeping your data and models protected.

Advancing AI research

Enabling breakthrough research. The next generation of AI models needs computational power that doesn't yet exist. We collaborate with research institutions to push the boundaries of what's computationally feasible, from sparse models to multimodal transformers.

Open ecosystem approach. Great AI happens when hardware and software ecosystems work together. We contribute to open-source tools, publish research, and engage with the ML community to accelerate innovation for everyone.
